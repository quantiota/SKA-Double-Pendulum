# VR Library

3D cubes of the learner's space, to explore on a desktop, a phone or in a VR headset.
Each cube shows what the SKA learner registers from one run of the IBM double pendulum.

## Cubes

IBM Double Pendulum Chaotic Dataset, 21 runs, 400 frames per second. Input: the return of v = cos(θ2 − θ1), scale 4.
Steps 3 to 3,500 (8.75 s). Open a cube and press **Enter VR** in the headset browser.

Two cubes per run: the cube (P), and the cube with H, the entropy, as the vertical axis; each also with a replay that follows the path step by step (400 steps/s = real time). Colours: v rising, v falling, v crossing 0 (the arms at 90°).
